# 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective date: 2026-11-08

适用于 Android 应用「记账本」（包名 `com.developproductivity.ledger`）。
This policy applies to the Android app "记账本" (Ledger, package `com.developproductivity.ledger`).

## 中文

1. **不收集任何个人信息。** 记账本不要求注册或登录，不读取通讯录、位置、相册等信息。
2. **数据只存在你的手机上。** 你记下的金额、分类、备注和日期，只保存在本机的应用存储里，不会上传到任何服务器，开发者也看不到。
3. **不联网传输数据，不含广告和第三方统计。** App 里没有广告 SDK，也没有分析或崩溃上报 SDK。
4. **删除数据。** 卸载 App，或在系统设置里清除 App 数据，会永久删除全部记录。
5. **儿童。** 本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不收集儿童信息。
6. **政策变更。** 政策有变化时会更新本页，并修改上面的生效日期。
7. **联系方式。** 有问题请在 <https://github.com/develop-productivity/ledger-app/issues> 提交 issue。

## English

1. **No personal data is collected.** The app needs no account or sign-in. It does not access your contacts, location, photos or other personal data.
2. **Your data stays on your device.** The amounts, categories, notes and dates you enter are stored only in the app's local storage on your phone. They are never uploaded to any server, and the developer cannot see them.
3. **No data is sent over the network. No ads, no analytics.** The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
4. **Deleting your data.** Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in system settings, permanently deletes all records.
5. **Children.** The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from them.
6. **Changes.** If this policy changes, this page will be updated together with the effective date above.
7. **Contact.** Please open an issue at <https://github.com/develop-productivity/ledger-app/issues>.
